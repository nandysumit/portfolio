Clients and executive stakeholders often describe him as a rare blend of strategist, transformation leader, and trusted advisor. Known for his candid counsel, customer-first mindset, and unwavering commitment to success, he builds trusted partnerships that extend far beyond project lifecycles.

What distinguishes Sumit is his relentless sense of ownership. His transparent leadership style, coupled with a focus on long-term value creation, has earned the confidence of executives, transformation teams, and customers across industries.

Legacy-to-SaaS HRMS Transformation for India's Largest Public Sector Bank
Transformation Advisor  ·  HRMS Architect  ·  AI Strategist
Challenge

One of India's largest public sector banks was operating a highly complex HR ecosystem supporting 250,000+ employees, 350,000+ pensioners, and over 150 HR services. Fragmented legacy systems, growing technical debt, and the absence of structured governance created operational inefficiencies, audit risks, and increasing complexity across payroll, pensions, claims, and workforce administration.

Approach

Led the modernization of the HR landscape through enterprise SaaS adoption, data governance, process standardization, and multi-vendor governance. Established audit-ready operating procedures, enhancement governance, and end-to-end traceability. Defined the AI roadmap for HR operations and introduced AI-powered service management capabilities.

Outcomes
  • 40%+ reduction in manual payroll interventions
  • Audit-ready governance and process controls established
  • Improved vendor accountability and issue-resolution predictability
  • AI roadmap approved for 600,000+ users across 150+ HR services
  • Scalable foundation created for future workforce transformation
